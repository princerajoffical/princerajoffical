### About Me

I'm a computer science student who enjoys understanding how things work beneath the surface and turning that curiosity into things people can actually use.

Most of my time goes into building for the web, experimenting with new technologies, and solving problems that challenge the way I think. I enjoy both the logic behind a system and the experience of using it, which is why I gravitate towards full-stack development.

I believe the best way to learn is to build, break things, figure out why they broke, and build them better. I'm still exploring, still experimenting, and always looking for the next problem worth solving.
